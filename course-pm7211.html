PM 7211  ·  Physics
Optics
2 Credits Theory 2nd Year
Learning Objectives
i
Discuss different kinds of aberration in Geometrical Optics and matrix analysis of lenses.
ii
Impart knowledge about interference with some applications of interference.
iii
Provide awareness about the mechanism of diffraction and its applications.
iv
Acquaint with different types of applications of interference phenomena.
Learning Outcomes
i
Learn the main cause of aberration in lenses and the matrix method used in matrix geometry of lens.
ii
Understand different types of aberrations produced and different mechanisms of minimizing aberrations.
iii
Realize the conditions for interference with special emphasis on temporal and spatial coherence.
iv
Learn how interference was examined by Thomas Young and the conditions for dark and bright fringes depending on path difference between two coherent rays.
v
Understand the conditions and types of diffraction; get familiar with single slit, double slit and multiple slit diffraction phenomena, especially diffraction grating and its use by a spectrometer.
