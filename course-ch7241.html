CH 7241  ·  Inorganic Chemistry
Chemistry of the Representative Elements
4 Credits Theory 2nd Year
Learning Objectives
i
Demonstrate an understanding of basics of s- and p-block elements.
ii
Discuss characteristics of alkali and alkaline earth metals, their compounds and the biological significance of Na, K, Mg and Ca.
iii
Explain large-scale preparation and properties of industrially important compounds — cement, plaster of Paris, NaOH, Na₂CO₃, NaHCO₃ etc.
iv
Present the principles of p-block element chemistry with emphasis on synthesis, structure, bonding and reaction mechanisms.
v
Describe the properties and uses of inert gases and underlying principles of making their compounds.
Learning Outcomes
i
Recognize s and p block elements and their characteristics.
ii
Describe physical and chemical properties of Group I–VII elements in the periodic table.
iii
Compare trends in reactivity in Groups I–VII and use atomic structures to explain reactivity variation within a group.
iv
Explain the chemistry of inert gases and their compounds, and uses of inert gases.
